The oldest coaster at Alton Towers, opened in 1992 with a layout by John
Wardley. It is a Mack Rides powered coaster, so there is no lift hill: the
motors are on board, and the long single train simply sets off and keeps
going.

The trick it plays is the second lap. Just as it seems to be pulling back into
the station it heads off round the circuit again, and on quiet days it has
been known to do a third. Along the way it shares a tunnel with the Congo River
Rapids.

It is small, friendly and slightly ramshackle in the best way, and it is the
right first coaster for anyone who has not tried one before.
