Sindbads Abenteuerreise opened in 2004 in Fantasiana's Afrika Orient area. It
is a dark ride on an I.E. Park ride system, with figures by Hofmann, and since
around 2010 its cars have been themed as little ships.

The story follows Sindbad the Sailor from the Arabian Nights, with a few Greek
and Egyptian myths mixed in. The cars rotate as they go, the ride spreads over
more than one floor, and along the way you meet the creatures of his travels,
including a cyclops.

It counts as a ghost train, but only just: the jump scares are mild, and it is
more storybook than scary. Do not miss the talking statue with the waterfall
pouring out of its mouth before you board.
