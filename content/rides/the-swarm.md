The Swarm is a Bolliger & Mabillard wing coaster, which means you sit out on
either side of the track with nothing above or below you. It opened in 2012,
and its story is the end of the world: a swarm of semi-organic machines has
attacked, and the ride takes you through what is left.

Everything around the track has already been wrecked: a half-destroyed church,
a crashed plane, an upturned ambulance, a fire engine sinking into the water and
a damaged helicopter. The ride threads past all of it closely, through a
billboard and right through the church tower.

For a few years the back rows faced backwards, but since 2016 everyone faces
forwards again. Sit on the right if you want the closest brush with the water
by the helicopter.
