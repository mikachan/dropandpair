Hero is a Zamperla Volare, a flying coaster that opened at Flamingo Land in 2013. You ride it lying face down, four to a car,
side by side, and it carries you up a spiral lift and through two heartline
rolls.

The numbers are modest: 15 metres up and around 40 km/h. The numbers are not
the point. The point is the riding position, which is famous for being
uncomfortable. You are held face down in a frame that presses into you in
all the wrong places, and every swoop and roll finds a new one.

It is short, and that is a mercy. Ride it once for the story, then go and do
something kinder to your body.
