A mountain coaster: you take the Möseralmbahn cable car up to the start, then
ride a small car down more than two kilometres of track, with 370 metres of
drop along the way. It runs through caves and tunnels and past a pirate ship
on its way down to the valley station in Fiss.

The joy of these is that you are driving. Brake as much or as little as you
like, and the ride is exactly as wild as you make it. Most people start
cautious and are flat out by the second go.

It does not run in the rain or in the wet, so check the sky before you plan
the day around it.
