Aquila is a Vekoma launch coaster at Mandoria, open since January 2025. It's
entirely indoors, so you can ride a proper launch in the middle of a Polish
winter.

Linear motors fire a 20-seat train from standing to 70 km/h in about three
seconds. There are no inversions. It doesn't need any.

It's smooth all the way round, and the launch is the best bit by miles.
