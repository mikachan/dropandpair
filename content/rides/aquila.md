Aquila opened in January 2025, a custom Vekoma launch coaster designed by
Camiel Bilsen, and it lives entirely indoors. That makes it a rare thing: a
proper launched ride you can do in the middle of a Polish winter.

The launch is linear-motor driven, taking a single 20-seat train of riders two
abreast from standing to 70 km/h in about three seconds. There are no
inversions, and it does not need any.

Moderate rather than brutal, and smooth throughout. Go for the launch, then go
again for it.
