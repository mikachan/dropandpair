The trick of Nemesis is that it is built down into the ground rather than up
into the sky, so there is no skyline shot and no gradual reveal. You queue
beside it, below it and occasionally underneath it, and the ride keeps
happening a few feet from your head while you wait.

What you get, riding it, is not a list of elements — it is a sustained refusal
to let go. There is no airtime pause, no coasting section, no moment where the
train is simply travelling from one thing to the next. It commits at the bottom
of the first drop and does not decommit until the brake run.

The walk-off is the surprising part. For something this relentless it is
remarkably smooth: the intensity is all in the forces, not in the hardware, so
you walk off shaken rather than rattled. Which is why the pairings below skew
towards _calm down_ rather than _lie down_.
