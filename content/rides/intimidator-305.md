Intimidator 305 opened in 2010, an Intamin giga coaster named after the NASCAR
driver Dale Earnhardt, "The Intimidator", and its own height of 305 feet. It
was the second giga coaster in North America, after Millennium Force.

A cable lift hauls you up at 45 degrees, then the drop falls 91 metres at 85 degrees,
and the train reaches 145 km/h. The first turn at the bottom was so intense
that some riders greyed out, so trim brakes were added to slow the train
down. Later the turn was rebuilt with a wider radius, the brakes came off, and
the ride got its full speed back.

The licence for the name ran out, so it was called Project 305 in 2024 and has
been Pantherian since 2025. The ride is the same; only the name has changed.
