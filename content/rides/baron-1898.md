The story is set in 1898. Baron Gustave Hooghmoed finds gold, and the Witte
Wieven, the White Women, warn him to leave it alone. He sends
his workers down the mine anyway, and you are the latest shift.

Baron 1898 is a Bolliger & Mabillard dive coaster, and it opened in 2015 as the
first of its kind in the Benelux. Just before the drop the train stops dead
over the shaft. Smoke rises, the Witte Wieven wail from the pit, and you are
left hanging there for long enough to regret every decision that brought you
here.

Then it lets go: 37.5 metres straight down into the mine, at up to 90 km/h,
and out through two inversions. It is short, smooth and beautifully staged,
and the pause is the bit everyone talks about afterwards.
