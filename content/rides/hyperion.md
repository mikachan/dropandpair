Hyperion is an Intamin mega coaster that opened in 2018, and its story is a
space mission to Hyperion, one of the moons of Saturn. Along the way the ship
meets a black hole and comes out through a vortex into another universe, where
it finds a twin of Earth.

The numbers are enormous: 77 metres tall, a drop of 82 metres at 85 degrees,
142 km/h and almost a kilometre and a half of track. It has a single
inversion, a dive-drop turnaround.

As of 2021 it was the tallest, fastest and longest coaster in Poland. It
is a long ride with a lot of speed in it, so brace yourself and enjoy the
journey.
