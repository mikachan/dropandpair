Eejanaika roughly means "ain't it great!", and it is a fourth-dimension coaster:
the seats sit out on wings either side of the track and spin a full 360 degrees,
forwards or backwards, while the train is doing its own thing.

It opened in 2006, a bigger, faster and longer cousin of X2 at Six Flags Magic
Mountain. The first drop is 65 metres at 89 degrees, and the top speed is 126
km/h.

It is counted as having 14 inversions, which ties it with The Smiler for the
world record, although 11 of them come from the seat flipping rather than the
track. Honestly, you will not be counting. You will be trying to work out
where the ground went.
