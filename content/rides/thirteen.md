Most of Thirteen is a family coaster through the trees, and that is the cover
story. The real ride is a few seconds in the dark, when the train stops, the
section of track it is sitting on lets go, and the whole lot drops about five
metres with you still on it.

It opened in 2010 as the world's first coaster with a vertical free-fall drop
of the track itself, and then carries on backwards through an underground
helix while you work out what just happened.

Once you know the trick it is a different ride: gentler, almost sweet. The
first time is the one that counts, so do not let anyone spoil it.
