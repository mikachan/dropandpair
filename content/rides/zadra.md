Zadra is the centrepiece of Energylandia's Dragon Zone, a whole themed area
built around dragons, and it opened there in 2019. The name means "splinter",
which suits a coaster with a wooden structure and steel track.

It is a Rocky Mountain Construction hybrid, and the first coaster built from
scratch with RMC's I-Box track rather than on top of an old wooden ride. It
climbs 63 metres, drops at 90 degrees, reaches 121 km/h and fits in three
inversions.

It ties with Iron Gwazi as the tallest RMC coaster in the world, and it has
been ranked among the best steel coasters anywhere. Ride it, then ride it again
from the other end of the train.
