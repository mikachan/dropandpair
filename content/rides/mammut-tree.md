Mammut Tree opened at Conny-Land in 2014, built by Swiss Rides. A 16-seat
gondola rides a cable from the lagoon up into a 21-metre "tree".

At first it's just a slightly bouncy cable car. Then you meet fire, water and
wind, and inside the tree there's a small free-fall.

It's not one for anxious small children. Everyone else will love the surprise.
