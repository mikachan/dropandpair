Mammut Tree opened at Conny-Land in 2014, built by the Swiss company Swiss
Rides, and the park bills it as a world premiere. A 16-seat gondola carries you
on a cable from the lagoon up into a 21-metre "tree" at the top.

On the way you meet fire, water and wind, and once you're inside the tree
there's a small free-fall effect. For the first stretch it looks like an
ordinary, slightly bouncy cable car, but it really isn't.

It came fourth for best family attraction in Europe at the 2014 European Star
Awards. It's not one for anxious small children, but it's a lovely surprise
for everyone else.
