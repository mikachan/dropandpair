Mammut Tree opened at Conny-Land in 2014, built by the Swiss company Swiss
Rides, and the park bills it as a world premiere. A 16-seat gondola carries you
on a cable from the lagoon up into a 21-metre "tree" at the top.

On the way you meet fire, water and wind, and once you are inside the tree
there is a small free-fall effect. That is the trick: for the first stretch it
looks like an ordinary, slightly bouncy cable car, and it is not.

It was voted the fourth-best family attraction in Europe at the 2014 European
Star Awards. Not for anxious small children, but a lovely surprise for
everyone else.
