Danse Macabre opened in 2024 on the site of Spookslot, Efteling's old haunted
castle, and it reuses some of its original props. The story is set in the
ruins of a 14th-century abbey, where a cursed orchestra plays Saint-Saëns's
_Danse macabre_ in a new orchestral arrangement.

It was the first ride to use Intamin's Dynamic Motion Stage: a big rotating
platform that turns and glides so it feels like you are dancing in time
with the music. It is half show and half ride, and it is not really trying to
scare you.

It has picked up awards, including a Golden Ticket for Best New Family
Attraction in 2025. Go for the music as much as the motion.
