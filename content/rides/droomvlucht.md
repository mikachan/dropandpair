Droomvlucht means "dreamflight", and that is exactly what it is: you sit in a
small open cabin hanging from the ceiling and float through five worlds — a
castle realm, an enchanted forest, a fairy garden, castles in the sky, and a
squelchy forest of trolls at the end.

It was designed by Ton van de Ven and opened in 1993. Nothing about it is fast
and none of it needs to be. The ride's one big move is a slow spiral down from
13 metres into the troll marshes.

About six minutes long, which is a luxury for a dark ride. Go slowly and look
up, down and sideways; there is far more in here than you will catch on one
ride.
