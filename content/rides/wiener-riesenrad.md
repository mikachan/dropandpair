The oldest ride here by about a century. It went up in 1897, built by the
British engineer Walter Basset Bassett, and it is still turning at the entrance
to the Prater.

It has not had an easy life. The wheel was badly damaged in the Second World
War and rebuilt with fifteen cabins instead of the original thirty. What is
left is slow, steady and quietly grand, and mostly an excuse to look at the
city from 65 metres up.

Nothing about it is a thrill, and that is the point. It is a ride for
looking out of, and talking to whoever you came with.
