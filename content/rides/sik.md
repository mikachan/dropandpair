Sik has an odd history for something so new. It was ordered by Hopi Hari in
Brazil in 2011, never built there, and sat in storage for years before
Flamingo Land took it on. It opened in 2022, named after the Scarborough
clothing brand SikSilk.

There is no theme and it does not need one. A cable lift takes you up and then
it gets straight to work: a loop, a cobra roll, a double corkscrew and a run of
heartline rolls, one straight after another.

It is not rough in the rattly sense, but ten inversions in a row has a
cumulative effect. Ride it once, properly, and then go and sit down.
