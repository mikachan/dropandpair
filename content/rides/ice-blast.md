The great mercy of a shot-and-drop tower is brevity. There is no lift hill to
think on, no slow climb, no hanging over an edge — you are sitting still, and
then you are not, and the transition takes no time at all.

What you buy with that is the view, and only for an instant. Up there you get
the coast in both directions and the sea filling most of your field of vision,
and then physics takes it away again before you have finished looking.

Silky finish, no back pain, no ringing ears. It is the most efficient thing in
the park: maximum event, minimum consequence. Pair it with something small and
cold and go straight round again.
