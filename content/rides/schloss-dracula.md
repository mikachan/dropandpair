Schloss Dracula is not strictly a ride: you walk through it. It opened in 2008
in Fantasiana's Schlosshof area, built by the park itself with figures by
Hofmann, and it tells the story of Count Dracula from the inside of his castle.

It runs all year and mixes live actors with animatronics, which is an
ambitious thing for a small park to pull off. Despite the subject, it is
pitched at families and older children and is not too frightening.

That changes at the park's Halloween event, when the scares are reportedly
turned up. Go in the summer for the story, and at Halloween if you
want the fright.
