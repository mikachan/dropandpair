This ride opened in 2000 as Superman: Ride of Steel, and for its first decade
it was regularly voted the best steel coaster in the world. In 2009 it was
rethemed as Bizarro: the track painted purple, flame effects added, and
on-board audio playing film quotes and rock music through the trains.

The Bizarro years ran until 2015. In 2016 it went back to being Superman,
repainted in its original colours and with the audio taken out. The ride is
still there, but this version of it is not.

Underneath any paint job it is the same thing: a 67-metre drop and a long, fast
run at well over 100 km/h. Bizarro just gave it a soundtrack.
