A seafront park, in the fullest sense: the sea is right there, the wind comes
off it, and the rides are stacked into each other so tightly that you are
usually standing underneath one while queueing for another.

It is the oldest-feeling park on this site and the best argued case that age is
a feature. The pairings here are seaside pairings — salt, vinegar, sugar, and a
sit down.
