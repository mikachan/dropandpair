Laid out as a tour of Europe, one themed area at a time, in a corner of
Baden-Württemberg near the edge of the Black Forest. The effect is of a park
that has decided to be extremely good at everything at once and then quietly
got on with it.

Pairings here tend towards the continental: cake you sit down for, and records
that take their time.
