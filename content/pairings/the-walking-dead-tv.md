description: >-
  Eleven seasons on AMC, from 2010 to 2022, developed by Frank Darabont from
  Robert Kirkman's comics. Survivors try to stay alive in a world overrun by
  the dead.
---

Starring Andrew Lincoln, Norman Reedus and a great many walkers. The zombies
are the backdrop; the real story is about the people, and how far they will go
to survive each other.

It is a long commitment. Start at the beginning and see how far you get.
