title: A long hot bath
pairingType: nightcap
description: >-
  Booked for the evening of a day that your spine will remember. Non-negotiable
  after anything with a coarse finish.
---

Some rides are wonderful and also assault you. Both things are allowed to be
true. The honest pairing for a coarse finish is not another ride; it is heat,
water, and an hour of lying still.

If the hotel has a bath rather than a shower, that is a factor worth weighing
when you book.
