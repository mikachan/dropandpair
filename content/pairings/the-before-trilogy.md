pairingType: before
description: >-
  Before Sunrise (1995), Before Sunset (2004) and Before Midnight (2013), from
  Richard Linklater, with Ethan Hawke and Julie Delpy. Two people, a lot of
  walking, and a great deal of talking.
---

Three films made nine years apart, each catching up with the same couple. Very
little happens and all of it matters: they walk, they talk, they argue about
whether any of it means anything, and the city does the rest.

Best watched in order, and best of all before a trip somewhere you would like
to wander about in.
