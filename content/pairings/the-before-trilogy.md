title: The Before trilogy
pairingType: before
description: >-
  Before Sunrise (1995), Before Sunset (2004) and Before Midnight (2013), from
  Richard Linklater, with Ethan Hawke and Julie Delpy. It's two people doing a
  lot of walking and a lot of talking.
---

Three films made nine years apart, each one catching up with the same
couple. Not much happens, but somehow it all feels important. They walk and
talk and argue about whether any of it means anything, and the city around
them is a big part of it too.

Watch them in order, ideally before a trip somewhere you'd like to wander
around.
