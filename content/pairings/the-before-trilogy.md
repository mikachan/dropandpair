title: The Before trilogy
pairingType: before
description: >-
  Before Sunrise (1995), Before Sunset (2004) and Before Midnight (2013), from
  Richard Linklater, with Ethan Hawke and Julie Delpy. Two people, lots of
  walking, and lots of talking.
---

Three films made nine years apart, each one catching up with the same
couple. Not much happens, but it all feels important. They walk, they talk,
they argue about whether any of it means anything, and the city around them
does the rest.

Watch them in order, ideally before a trip somewhere you'd like to wander
around.
