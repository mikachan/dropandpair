title: A proper pot of tea, sitting down
pairingType: wind-down
description: >-
  A proper pot of tea, a comfy chair, and ten minutes where you don't have to be
  anywhere.
---

This is probably my most underrated pairing, and the one people skip the most.
After a ride that's thrown you about, sit down somewhere comfy and let your
heart rate come back down before you join another queue.

Tea's perfect for this because you can't drink it in a hurry!
