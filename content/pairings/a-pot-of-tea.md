title: A proper pot of tea, sitting down
pairingType: palate-cleanser
description: >-
  A pot, not a paper cup. A chair, not a wall. Ten minutes in which nothing is
  scheduled.
---

The most underrated pairing on the site and the one most often skipped. After
something that has rearranged your insides, the correct next move is not
another queue — it is to sit somewhere with a back on it and let your pulse
come down to a speed you can think at.

Tea is specified because it takes long enough to drink that you cannot rush it.
That is the entire mechanism.
