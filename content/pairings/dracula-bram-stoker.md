title: Dracula by Bram Stoker
pairingType: read
description: >-
  The 1897 novel that started it all, told through letters, diaries and
  newspaper cuttings, from a castle in Transylvania to the seaside at Whitby.
---

It opens with a young English solicitor travelling to a castle in Transylvania
on business, and his journal slowly working out that his host is not what he
seems. Those first chapters are as good as gothic horror gets.

Because it is all letters and diaries, it reads in short pieces: ideal for the
hotel, the flight home, or a dark winter evening.
