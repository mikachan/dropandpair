description: >-
  The 1897 novel that started it all, told through letters, diaries and
  newspaper cuttings, from a castle in Transylvania to the seaside at Whitby.
---

It starts with a young English solicitor travelling to a castle in
Transylvania on business, and his journal slowly working out that his host
isn't quite what he seems. I think those first chapters are some of the best
gothic horror there is.

Because it's all letters and diaries, it's easy to read in short bursts, so
it's great for the hotel, the flight home, or a dark winter evening.
