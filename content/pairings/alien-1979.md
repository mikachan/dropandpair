The pairing is a mood, not a trivia answer: the specific dread of something
large and unknowable in a wet dark space, taking its time. If a ride has left
you looking over your shoulder, this is the film that agrees with you.

Watch it late, watch it loud, and do not watch it in the hotel room the night
before an early park opening.
