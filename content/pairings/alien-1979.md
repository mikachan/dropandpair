I've paired this one for the mood rather than any real connection: that
feeling of something huge and unknown hiding somewhere dark and wet, and taking
its time. If a ride's left you looking over your shoulder, this is a good one
to watch next.

Watch it late and turn it up, but maybe don't watch it in the hotel the night
before an early start at the park.
