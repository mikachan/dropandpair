title: Hyperion by Dan Simmons

description: >-
  Dan Simmons's 1989 science fiction novel, which won the Hugo Award. Seven
  pilgrims travel to the Time Tombs on the planet Hyperion, where the Shrike is
  waiting.
---

It's set out like The Canterbury Tales. Seven pilgrims are travelling to the
Time Tombs on Hyperion, and on the way each of them tells the story of why they
came. The stories cover faith, war, art, loss and revenge, and I think together
they make one of the best science fiction novels out there.

It carries on in The Fall of Hyperion (1990), so it's worth having that one
ready before you finish.
