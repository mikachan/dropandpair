title: The Gold Rush (1925)
pairingType: watch
description: >-
  Charlie Chaplin's silent comedy, which he wrote, directed and starred in as
  the Lone Prospector, chasing his fortune in the Klondike Gold Rush.
---

It has three scenes everyone remembers: the Tramp cooking and eating his own
boot, the dance of the bread rolls on forks, and a cabin wobbling on the edge
of a cliff in a blizzard. It's funny, sad and surprisingly tense.

Chaplin re-released it in 1942 with music and his own narration, and I think
both versions are brilliant.
