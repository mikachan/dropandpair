title: Après-ski music
pairingType: listen
description: >-
  Schlager, oompah brass and choruses made for a room full of people in ski
  boots to sing along to. It's loud, daft and really hard to sit still to.
---

This is what you'll hear in a mountain bar at four in the afternoon. Nobody
listens to it for the lyrics. It's music for joining in with, badly, while
someone bangs the table in time.

It works just as well in summer, with walking shoes instead of ski boots, and
I think it's even better on the drive back down the valley.
