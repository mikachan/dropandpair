title: Après-ski music
pairingType: listen
description: >-
  Schlager, oompah brass and choruses built to be sung by a room full of people
  in ski boots. Loud, daft and impossible to sit still to.
---

The soundtrack of the mountain bar at four in the afternoon. Nobody listens to
it for the lyrics, and nobody needs to. It is music for joining in with,
badly, while somebody bangs the table in time.

It works just as well in summer, with the boots swapped for walking shoes, and
it works best of all on the drive back down the valley.
