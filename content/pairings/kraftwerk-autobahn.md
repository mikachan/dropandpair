  Twenty-two minutes of German motorway, in the best possible sense. For the
  drive, or the long walk back to the car park.
---

Music that moves at a constant speed and is entirely comfortable about it. It
suits rides with long, smooth, unhurried stretches — the ones whose pleasure is
momentum rather than violence.

Best appreciated as the title track in full, rather than the single edit.
