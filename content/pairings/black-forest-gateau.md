title: Black Forest gateau, sat down properly
pairingType: eat-and-drink
description: >-
  Cherries, cream, chocolate and a fork. Ordered at a table, with coffee, at an
  hour that is frankly too late for cake.
---

A regional pairing and an unapologetically slow one. The rule is that it must
be eaten sitting at a table with a saucer involved — not carried, not queued
with, not eaten walking.

Order coffee alongside it. You will need the contrast.
