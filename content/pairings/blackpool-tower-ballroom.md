title: The Blackpool Tower Ballroom
pairingType: do-next
description: >-
  A ballroom with a sprung floor, an organ, and a room temperature of about
  1910. Twenty minutes from the park on foot.
---

The correct decompression from a day of steel and shouting is a room built for
the exact opposite: gilded, deliberate, and full of people dancing properly in
the middle of the afternoon.

You do not have to dance. Sitting at the edge with a drink, watching people who
clearly can, is the pairing.
