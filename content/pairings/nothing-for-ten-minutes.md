id: nothing-for-ten-minutes
title: Ten minutes of nothing
pairingType: before
description: >-
  Get there early, find somewhere you can see the ride, and spend a bit of time
  building up to it before you join the queue.
---

I think rides with a big reputation are better when you've given yourself
time to get a bit nervous first. Find a spot where you can watch the trains go
round, or hear them without seeing them, and stay there until you're slightly
reluctant to go.

It's not the most efficient way to spend your day, but it makes that first
ride feel really special.
