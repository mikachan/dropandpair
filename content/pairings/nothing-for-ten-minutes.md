id: nothing-for-ten-minutes
title: Ten minutes of nothing
pairingType: before
description: >-
  Arrive early, stand somewhere you can see the ride, and let the anticipation
  do its work before you join the queue.
---

Rides with a reputation are better when you have let the reputation settle on
you first. Find the spot where you can watch a train go round, or hear it
without seeing it, and stay there until you are slightly reluctant.

This is the opposite of a single-rider queue. It is deliberately inefficient,
and it makes the first ride of the day the best one you will get.
