title: The Wicker Man (1973)
pairingType: watch
description: >-
  Robin Hardy's folk horror, with Edward Woodward and Christopher Lee. A police
  sergeant goes to a remote Scottish island to look for a missing girl, and the
  islanders are far too cheerful about it.
---

A film that is sunny, tuneful and deeply wrong all at once. The island of
Summerisle is beautiful, the locals sing a great deal, and the sense that
something is being prepared for you builds so slowly that you barely notice it
arriving.

Watch the original. Do not let anyone tell you how it ends.
