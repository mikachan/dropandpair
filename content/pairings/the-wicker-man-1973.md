title: The Wicker Man (1973)
pairingType: watch
description: >-
  Robin Hardy's folk horror, with Edward Woodward and Christopher Lee. A police
  sergeant goes to a remote Scottish island to look for a missing girl, and the
  islanders are far too cheerful about it.
---

It's sunny, full of songs and really unsettling all at the same time. The
island of Summerisle is beautiful, the locals sing a lot, and the feeling that
something's being planned for you creeps up so slowly that you barely notice
it.

Make sure you watch the original, and don't let anyone tell you how it ends.
